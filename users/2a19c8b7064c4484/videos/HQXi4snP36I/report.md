## Title: The AI Agent Every Company is About to Build | Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch

Guillermo Rauch, CEO of Vercel, discusses the emerging landscape of AI agents within companies. He shares insights from Vercel's internal 'V' agent and introduces 'Eve,' a framework for building custom agents.

### Introduction: The Rise of AI Agents (00:00 - 01:20)
Guillermo Rauch, CEO of Vercel, discusses the growing importance of AI agents in business and introduces Vercel's internal agent, highlighting its capabilities and impact.

### AI Agents as Killer Applications (01:20 - 04:00)
Rauch explains how AI agents are becoming killer applications, drawing parallels to past technological shifts like personal computers and mobile phones, with a focus on coding agents and 'run your company better' agents.

### Vercel's Internal 'V' Agent (04:00 - 06:23)
The discussion delves into Vercel's internal 'V' agent, an omni-present intelligence in Slack that helps employees with tasks, acting as a router to various specialized sub-agents.

### Challenges in Implementing AI Agents (06:23 - 08:38)
The host asks about the difficulties of implementing AI agents for teams, particularly regarding permissions and integrating personal skills, prompting Rauch to explain Vercel's approach.

### The 'Soul' of an Agent: Eve Framework (08:38 - 11:58)
Rauch introduces 'Eve,' Vercel's framework for building agents, emphasizing the concept of 'soul.md' files that define an agent's genesis and the importance of giving agents a secure, serverless computing environment.

### God Agent vs. Team of Agents (11:58 - 14:15)
The conversation addresses whether to build one 'god agent' or a team of agents, with Rauch advocating for a central routing agent that manages access and delegates to specialized tools based on context.

### Building and Maintaining Your Own Agent (14:15 - 17:35)
Rauch explains how to start building an Eve agent by defining its instructions, adding tools (like WordPress integration), and setting up communication channels, while also managing guardrails and human approvals.

### Proactive Agents and Self-Improvement (17:35 - 20:56)
The discussion shifts to the concept of proactive agents that perform tasks autonomously and the importance of self-improvement for agents, including methods for evaluation and feedback.

### Accessibility of Agent Building (20:56 - 23:44)
Rauch believes agent building will become democratized, with everyone able to create basic agents, while technical experts focus on core system loops, data access, and security.

### Getting Started with Eve (23:44 - 26:08)
Rauch encourages listeners to try Eve.dev, connect an agent to a chat medium, and automate a 'toil task' as a starting point, sharing an example of Vercel's agent assisting with product changelogs.

### Future of AI: Cost and Speed (26:08 - 29:28)
The conversation concludes with Rauch's excitement about the decreasing cost and increasing speed of AI models, likening the current state to the early days of the iPhone.

### Model Agnosticism and Optimization (29:28 - 32:53)
Rauch highlights the benefit of model agnosticism in Eve, allowing users to choose the best models for different tasks (fast for interactive, accurate for asynchronous) and benefit from market competition.
